## 1. WORLD GENRE & AESTHETIC

- **Genre Blend**: **Science Fiction + Steampunk + Spiritual Realm**  
- **Aesthetic**: Victorian-era steampunk with brass gears, steam-powered mechanisms, airships, clockwork automatons, but infused with **spiritual reality** where the Veil bleeds into the physical world  
- **Technology Level**:  
  - **Steam-Powered**: Airships, mechanized forges, gear-driven weapons  
  - **Brass & Copper**: All machinery has verdigris patina, engraved with ancient runes  
  - **Clockwork**: Automatons, timepieces, mechanical guardians  
  - **Victorian Influence**: Waistcoats, pocket watches, top hats, corsets, but with fantasy grit  
- **Spiritual Integration**: The Veil is **physically visible** — emerald mists, floating entities, spiritual wounds manifest as scars/glowing veins  
- **Visual Palette**:  
  | Element | Colors | Textures |  
  |---------|--------|----------|  
  | Physical World | Brass, copper, rust, fog, ash | Gearwork, leather, stone, wood |  
  | Veil | Emerald, black, silver | Shimmering mist, shadow, void |  
  | Sparks | Blue flame | Crackling energy, heat waves |  
  | Nephilim | Obsidian, crimson, bone | Scales, horns, jagged metal |

## 2. COSMOLOGY — THE FOUR REALMS

### 1. Divine Realm (Unseen Foundation)
- **Description**: The Almighty’s throne, inaccessible to mortals  
- **Manifestation**: Three Aspects (Trinity hint):  
  | Aspect | Role | Physical Echo |  
  |--------|------|---------------|  
  | **Oden** (Justice) | Prophetic guidance | Zionhold's law tablets |  
  | **Anarrak** (Strength) | Spark power source | Ironcrags' forges |  
  | **Gwendolyn** (Grace) | Healing, revival | Brightspire's refuge |  
- **Access**: Elnor's visions only  

### 2. The Veil (Spiritual Realm — **Equally Real as Physical**)
- **Description**: **Overlapping layer of Danarsia** — not separate dimension  
- **Appearance**:  
  - **Seductive Phase**: Emerald mists, radiant figures cloaked in green/silver  
  - **Grotesque Phase**: Fanged maws, hollow eyes, bleeding shadows, claws  
- **Physics**:  
  | Physical Action | Veil Effect |  
  |-----------------|-------------|  
  | Spark shield | Burns Watcher forms |  
  | Sword strike | Cuts spiritual flesh |  
  | Soul wound | Physical scar + black veins |  
- **Key Mechanics**:  
  - **Temptation**: Watchers appear as "angels of light" (2 Corinthians 11:14)  
  - **Possession**: Black veins, glowing eyes, Watcher voice  
  - **Soul-Binding**: Victim freezes, soul trapped until freed by spark  
- **Anchors**: 12 regions have Veil counterparts (e.g., Thalrune's shrine = Veil anchor)
